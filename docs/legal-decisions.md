# Open legal and abuse decisions

## Evidence and erasure

The interim defaults in `private.safety_retention` are 365 days after review for child-safety reports and 90 days after review for other reports. Pending reports stay in the review queue. The report can retain account identifiers, the reported snapshot and copies of referenced files after account deletion. An account or report legal hold suspends erasure where the hold applies. Review holds, pending reports, backups and log retention on a defined schedule.

Confirm which evidence must be preserved by law under GDPR Article 17(3)(b), or is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims under Article 17(3)(e). Set the basis, scope, retention start, review owner, release authority and response to the data subject for each case. Confirm separate safeguards for sensitive data and data relating to offences. The operator must record the authority and date for each hold in the restricted case record. The historical policy acceptance import records when the service imported declarations from user-writable metadata. Confirm whether those accounts need to accept again as a condition of launch. [GDPR Articles 5, 6, 9, 10 and 17](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng).

## Upload scanning and reporting duties

Select and contract with Microsoft PhotoDNA or Thorn Safer after confirming supported media, false-positive handling, minimum data transfer, processor terms, jurisdiction, security and retention. Cloudflare's CSAM Scanning would require the files to pass through Cloudflare. Member uploads currently go directly to Supabase Storage. Keep `scan_new_uploads` off until an approved adapter, lawful basis and handling procedure are in place. The `SAFETY_SCAN_PROVIDER=noop` setting performs no detection. Private notes' text is outside the upload scanning pipeline. [Microsoft PhotoDNA](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/photodna), [Thorn Safer Match](https://www.thorn.org/blog/safer-match/), [Cloudflare CSAM Scanning](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cache/reference/csam-scanning/).

Confirm who must report, what threshold and deadline apply, and the permitted evidence transfer. For US operations or US users, determine whether 18 U.S.C. § 2258A applies and whether registration with NCMEC's CyberTipline is required. For Italy, confirm the escalation path to the Polizia Postale or CNCPO and the DSA Article 18 procedure. Agree on coverage, on-call escalation and any restrictions on notifying the affected account. Automated scan matches alert the internal safety team. Any communication to authorities or the affected account requires the approved case procedure. [NCMEC guidance](https://www.missingkids.org/content/gpp-ncmec/us/en/policy-advocacy/reporting-by-online-platforms.html), [Polizia Postale and CNCPO](https://www.commissariatodips.it/docs/BROCHURE_5_MAGGIO_2024.pdf), [Digital Services Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj/eng).

## DSA contact, notices and decisions

Confirm the service's classification, the staffed English and Italian contact, and the person who can act on notices and orders. The public form implements intake, electronic receipt and a queue for decisions. Confirm the process for timely decisions, statements of reasons, redress, notice-handling records and applicable transparency obligations. Agree how to verify delivery, correct failed notifications and operate the inbox before publishing a service commitment. [Digital Services Act Articles 11, 12, 16, 17 and 18](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj/eng).
